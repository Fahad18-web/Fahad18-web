### 2. Heal2RiseBook — Mental Health & Social Welfare Platform

**Final Year Project | Grade: A**

Heal2RiseBook is a social welfare platform designed to connect individuals experiencing mental health and personal challenges with relevant NGOs that can provide support.

The platform aims to make mental health assistance more accessible by creating a structured connection between individuals, NGOs and support professionals.

**Core Concept & Features**
- Connect individuals with relevant NGOs for assistance.
- Support individuals experiencing anxiety, depression, hopelessness and marital issues.
- Enable NGOs to assign appropriate team members, including psychiatrists and counsellors.
- Provide an administrative verification process for platform participants.
- Manage support cases through a structured workflow.
- Allow case closure after satisfaction is confirmed by both the individual and NGO.

**Technology:** PHP

**Project Achievement:** Successfully completed as a Final Year Project and awarded Grade A.
